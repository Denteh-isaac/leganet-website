Our Services

Starlink Installation

Professional Starlink installation for homes, offices, schools, hospitals, hotels, hostels and business environments that require dependable high-speed internet. LEGANET handles site assessment, mounting, cabling, router configuration, signal optimization and network integration to ensure a stable and efficient internet experience from the start.

  • Site survey and installation planning
  • Dish mounting, cabling and equipment setup
  • Router configuration and network integration
  • Signal optimization and coverage planning
  • Support for homes, institutions and commercial spaces

Community Hotspot Business

End-to-end hotspot business setup for hostels, communities, apartment blocks, campuses and public internet operators who want to provide and monetize internet access. We don't stop at installation — we help set up the systems that make the hotspot business usable, manageable and commercially practical.

  • Community hotspot network deployment
  • Captive portal setup for user login and access
  • Voucher system for timed or package-based access
  • Mobile money integration for customer payments
  • PPPoE configuration and session management
  • User access control and troubleshooting support

Ideal for: hostels, neighborhoods, apartment blocks, campuses, guest facilities, event spaces and Wi-Fi entrepreneurs.

Networking

Structured network design, deployment and support for companies, banks, schools, hospitals, hotels and institutions that depend on secure and stable connectivity. From small office networks to larger institutional setups, we build systems that support productivity, communication and operational continuity.

  • Router, switch and access point deployment
  • Structured cabling and network organization
  • VLAN setup and internal network segmentation
  • Network optimization and troubleshooting
  • Scalable solutions for institutions and businesses

Home & Office Wi-Fi Installation

Wi-Fi systems designed for strong coverage, stable performance and smooth connectivity across homes, apartments, offices, hostels and commercial spaces. We assess coverage requirements, user density and building conditions to create Wi-Fi systems that minimize dead zones and improve reliability.

  • Coverage improvement for weak signal areas
  • Solutions for homes, offices, hostels and shared spaces
  • Reliable setup for work, streaming, meetings and daily operations
