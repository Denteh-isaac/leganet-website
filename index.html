About Leganet

Founded in Accra, Leganet is on a mission to bridge the digital divide across Ghana through cutting-edge satellite technology and local expertise.

Our Story

Leganet Internet Solutions was born from a simple observation: millions of Ghanaians lack access to reliable, high-speed internet. What started as a small Starlink installation service in Accra has grown into a comprehensive internet solutions provider.

Our team of certified technicians combines deep local knowledge with cutting-edge technology. We understand the unique challenges of connectivity in Ghana — from power fluctuations to tropical weather.

Today, Leganet serves over 500 clients across 12+ locations, from bustling Accra neighbourhoods to remote northern communities. We're proud to be at the forefront of Ghana's digital transformation.
